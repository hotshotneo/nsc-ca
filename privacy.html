Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Neeraj Sonigara & Co., Chartered Accountants ("we") uses WhatsApp and email to receive documents from clients and to send them queries and reminders about their tax and compliance work. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

Your name, mobile number, email address, and the documents and messages you send us (for example bank statements, Form 16, invoices and tax notices).

How we use it

Only to prepare and file your returns, audits and other assignments you give us, to tell you which documents are still needed, and to send you reminders. We do not use your information for advertising and we do not sell or share it with anyone, except government portals and authorities where filing requires it.

Where it is kept

Documents are stored in our secure office cloud storage with access limited to the firm. Messages are delivered through the WhatsApp Business Platform operated by Meta.

How long we keep it

For as long as the law requires us to keep working papers for your assignments, after which it is deleted.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to stop WhatsApp messages, or to correct or delete your information, by replying STOP or messaging us on WhatsApp at +91 88301 79149, or calling +91 94225 17827.
